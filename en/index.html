01About

I work where the conversation has to happen without an interpreter

An international project rests on three things: a signed agreement, a delegation that arrives and leaves without a hitch, and a report the state client accepts. I am accountable for all three.

Portuguese is my main working language: MGIMO, first foreign language, C1 across diplomatic, legal and commercial vocabulary. I speak to the Latin American side directly, without an interpreter, and that noticeably shortens approval timelines.

The other half of the work is operational and financial: visit programmes, visas, contractors, venues, payment processing, closing documents and reporting. I am equally at ease negotiating with a ministry and reconciling source documents worth nine million roubles.
